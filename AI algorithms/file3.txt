Blockchain technology is a decentralized digital ledger. It records transactions across many computers. Blockchain ensures transparency and security. Bitcoin was the first application of blockchain. Blockchain eliminates the need for intermediaries. It offers faster and cheaper transactions. Each block contains a cryptographic hash of the previous block. This links blocks securely. Public blockchains are open to anyone. Private blockchains are restricted to certain users. Smart contracts are programs that run on blockchain. They execute automatically when conditions are met. Ethereum introduced smart contracts widely. Blockchain has applications beyond cryptocurrency. Supply chain management benefits from blockchain transparency. Healthcare can use blockchain for secure patient records. Voting systems based on blockchain could improve trust. Challenges for blockchain include scalability and energy consumption. Proof of work and proof of stake are consensus mechanisms. NFTs, or non-fungible tokens, are a recent blockchain innovation. Many industries are exploring blockchain's potential. It is important to address regulatory challenges. Blockchain continues to evolve rapidly.
