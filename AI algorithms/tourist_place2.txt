The Great Wall of China is one of the world’s greatest architectural feats. Stretching over 21,000 kilometers, it was built to protect Chinese states from invasions. Constructed over several centuries, it is not a single wall but a series of walls, fortifications, and watchtowers. The wall runs through some of the most rugged terrain, including mountains and deserts. Today, it stands as a UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the most visited tourist destinations in the world.
