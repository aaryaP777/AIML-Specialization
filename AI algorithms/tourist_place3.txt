Machu Picchu is an ancient Inca city located in the Andes Mountains of Peru. It was built in the 15th century and later abandoned. The site was rediscovered by American historian Hiram Bingham in 1911. Machu Picchu is famous for its sophisticated dry-stone construction and panoramic views. It is thought to have been a royal estate or a religious site. Visitors can hike the Inca Trail to reach it, making it a popular adventure destination.
