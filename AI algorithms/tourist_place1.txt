The Eiffel Tower is one of the most famous landmarks in Paris. Standing at 330 meters tall, it offers a breathtaking view of the city. Visitors can take an elevator ride to the top or enjoy the view from the second floor. Originally designed by Gustave Eiffel for the 1889 World’s Fair, it was initially criticized but soon became a symbol of France. The tower is made of wrought iron and weighs about 10,100 tons. Every evening, the Eiffel Tower is illuminated, creating a magical sight.
