I wasn’t expecting much from this movie, but I was pleasantly surprised. The acting was solid, and the characters were well-developed. There were moments of humor that felt natural, and the film had a good balance of lightheartedness and drama. The visual effects were stunning, particularly the scenes involving the city’s skyline. The storyline was a bit predictable, but the charming performances of the cast made it enjoyable. The soundtrack added to the emotional depth of the film, especially during key moments. The pacing was a bit slow at times, but overall, it was an enjoyable watch. It’s not the most original film, but it is certainly entertaining. If you're looking for a feel-good movie to watch with family, this is a good pick. It might not win any awards, but it’s worth a watch for its entertainment value.
